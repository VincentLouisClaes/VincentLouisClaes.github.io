Research

Barred Galaxies & Boxy/Peanut Bulges

First-author project analyzing a suite of N-body simulations of barred galaxies to study the connection between vertical boxy/peanut morphology and bar kinematics. I use photometric modeling and automated pipelines to measure structural parameters and explore correlations with bar pattern speed.

Protostellar Infall Simulations

Earlier work with Lee Hartmann modeling velocity fields in protostellar infall simulations. I developed 3D visualizations to examine accretion flows and presented this work at the AAS.

Data Science & Institutional Analytics

As a Data Management Fellow at Michigan’s Center for Academic Innovation, I analyze large enrollment datasets, build dashboards, and develop metrics for course and learning technology engagement.
